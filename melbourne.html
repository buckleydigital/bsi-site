Melbourne Homeowners Ask Us…

Honest answers to the questions we hear most

☁️ "Does solar even work in cloudy Melbourne?"
Absolutely. Solar panels generate electricity from daylight, not direct sunlight. Melbourne's 2,200+ annual sunshine hours — plus many bright cloud-diffused days — give most systems an excellent return. Our matched installers size your system correctly for VIC conditions.
💸 "How much does it actually cost after the VIC rebate?"
After the Solar Homes Program rebate (up to $1,400*), a quality 6.6kW system in Melbourne typically costs $3,500–$5,500 installed. Your matched installer will give you an exact figure with no hidden fees.
🔧 "How do I know the installer is trustworthy?"
Every installer we match you with is CEC-accredited, fully licensed, insured, and has been independently reviewed. We only work with the top-rated operators in your Melbourne suburb — no cowboys, ever.
⏱️ "How long does installation take?"
Most Melbourne residential solar installations are completed in a single day. From submitting your quote to panels on the roof, most of our customers are up and running within 2–4 weeks.
🤔 "Is there any obligation after I get a quote?"
None whatsoever. Getting a quote through Better Solar Installers is completely free and zero-obligation. If you love the quote, great. If not, no pressure — we won't hassle you.
📝 "Do I qualify for the VIC Solar Homes rebate?"
The rebate is generally available to owner-occupiers earning under $180,000 combined annually, with an eligible property. Your matched installer will confirm your exact eligibility as part of your free quote.
